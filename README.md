# 🛒 Grocery Store Management System | SQL Project

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Grocery Store Management System** is a SQL-based project designed to manage and analyze grocery store operations. It focuses on customers, products, suppliers, categories, employees, orders, and order details.

This project uses SQL queries to extract meaningful insights from grocery store data, helping businesses understand customer purchasing patterns, product performance, sales trends, supplier contributions, and employee performance.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Grocery stores maintain large amounts of data related to sales, customers, products, and inventory. Analyzing this data manually can be difficult.

This project uses SQL to transform raw data into useful business insights and support better decision-making related to sales, inventory, customer behavior, and store operations.

## 🗂️ Database Tables
The project consists of seven interconnected tables:

1. **Supplier** – Stores supplier information.
2. **Categories** – Maintains product category details.
3. **Products** – Contains product information, prices, suppliers, and categories.
4. **Customers** – Stores customer details.
5. **Employees** – Maintains employee information.
6. **Orders** – Records customer orders.
7. **Order Details** – Stores products, quantities, and prices associated with each order.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **MySQL** – Database management and querying
- **SQL** – Data analysis and business insights

## 📊 Key Analysis Questions
- How many unique customers have placed orders?
- What are the total and average purchase values per customer?
- Who are the top 5 customers by total purchase amount?
- How many products exist in each category?
- Which products have the highest sales volume by quantity?
- On which dates were the most orders placed?
- How do order patterns vary between weekdays and weekends?
- Which suppliers contribute the most to total product sales by revenue?
- How does the unit price vary across products and orders?

## 📈 Business Insights
The analysis helps to:
- Identify active customers and high-value customers.
- Understand popular products and category distribution.
- Recognize high-demand dates and shopping patterns.
- Evaluate supplier contributions to sales revenue.
- Compare product prices across orders.
- Monitor employee order-handling performance.

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Maintain sufficient stock for popular products.
- Understand customer spending behavior to improve customer retention.
- Strengthen relationships with valuable suppliers.
- Monitor daily and monthly sales trends.
- Evaluate employee performance using order data.
- Use regular SQL reports to support business decisions.
